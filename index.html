⚙️ How RaisoVoice Solves Institutional Stagnation
Start Filing →
🥷

1. Zero-PII Anonymous Shield

Students can file complaints without submitting name, roll number, or institutional email. No retaliatory bias or grading consequences. You receive an encrypted private tracking key.

⛓️

2. SHA-256 Cryptographic Chain

Every complaint creates a Genesis block. Every subsequent administrative note, status update, or inspection seals a new cryptographically chained block. No official can silently edit or delete history.

⏱️

3. Strict SLA Deadlines

Complaints have binding SLAs: Urgent (24h), High (48h), Medium (72h), Low (120h). If an assigned officer fails to resolve the issue within deadline, the ticket automatically escalates to the Dean of Student Welfare.

📊

4. Public Transparency Dashboard

Campus-wide institutional responsiveness metrics are published openly. Students see turnaround rates for hostel wardens, mess committees, computer center, and estate engineers.

📢 Recent Campus Redressal Activity

Live audit actions recorded on the institutional ledger. Student privacy strictly protected.
